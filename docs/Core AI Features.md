### Core AI Features

* **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG):** AI that securely reads proprietary company data (like uploaded PDFs, markdown files, or internal wikis) to provide truthful, grounded answers without hallucinating generic responses.
* **Predictive Lead Scoring:** Machine learning models that evaluate CRM pipelines by analyzing historical win rates, engagement signals, and firmographics to assign dynamic conversion probabilities to active deals.
* **Generative Content Engines:** AI that automatically drafts follow-up emails for sales reps or generates multi-choice quizzes for internal learning modules.
* **Intelligent Interceptors:** Automated system guards that monitor usage limits (like AI credit exhaustion or seat limits) and instantly route users to billing or upgrade portals without manual intervention.

### Use Cases by Department

| Department | Application | Business Impact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Sales (CRM)** | **Automated Deal Scoring & Outreach:** Sales reps click a button to score a deal's likelihood of closing, and the AI drafts a context-aware email based on the current Kanban board stage. | Prioritizes high-value prospects and reduces time spent writing manual outreach. |
| **Training (LMS)** | **Instant Quiz Generation:** When a manager creates a new training lesson, the AI reads the course text and automatically generates a multi-choice assessment to test comprehension. | Accelerates course creation and enforces learning standards without manual test writing. |
| **Customer Support** | **Tier 1 Knowledge Deflection:** A RAG chatbot sits on the front end, answering user FAQs instantly by querying the uploaded documentation index. | Reduces average handling time and escalates only complex, edge-case problems to human agents. |
| **Operations** | **Smart Billing Locks:** The system monitors AI usage; if a workspace exhausts its credit allowance, the AI intercepts the API request and triggers a soft-lock UI pointing to Stripe. | Protects infrastructure costs and drives automated revenue expansion. |
