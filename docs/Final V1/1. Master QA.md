# 🧪 Master QA Test Plan: V1 Release (Junior QA Edition)

**Welcome to the QA Team!** This document is your step-by-step guide to testing our new application. Your job is to act like a real user and try to make the system break. If everything works exactly as written under **"Expected Result,"** the test passes. If it doesn't, write down what happened and report it as a bug.

### 🛠️ Before You Start (Setup Checklist)

1. **Start the App:** Make sure the backend developers have started the API, and your frontend server is running.
2. **Open the App:** Open Google Chrome and go to `http://localhost:4200`.
3. **Open Developer Tools (Important!):** Press `F12` on your keyboard (or right-click the page and select "Inspect"). Click on the **"Network"** tab at the top. This helps you see if the app is successfully talking to the database.
4. **Incognito Mode:** You will frequently need to test as different users. Use an "Incognito" or "Private" browsing window (`Ctrl + Shift + N`) so your login sessions don't get mixed up.

---

### 🎭 The User Roles (Who you are acting as)

We have three types of users. You will need a test email/password for each from your developers:

* **`SUPER_ADMIN`**: The master builder. This is our internal company account. Can see hidden system tools.
* **`TENANT_OWNER`**: The boss of a customer company. They can manage billing, taxes, and invite their workers.
* **`DOMAIN_MEMBER`**: A normal worker (like a sales rep or student). They have restricted access.

---

## 🟢 Phase 1: Security & Setup Gates (Super Admin)

### Test 1.1: The Hidden Onboarding Gate

*Why we test this: We don't want random people creating spam accounts on our public website.*

* **Role:** Anonymous (Make sure you are logged out)
* **Step 1:** Go to `http://localhost:4200/onboard` in your browser.
* **Expected Result:** You should NOT see a signup form. You MUST see a message saying something like "Signup is invite-only. Contact sales."
* **Step 2:** Now, change the URL in the address bar to include our secret code: `http://localhost:4200/onboard?code=SECRET123` and press Enter.
* **Expected Result:** The registration form should magically appear.

### Test 1.2: Super Admin Tenant Creator

*Why we test this: Super Admins need a backdoor to manually create companies.*

* **Role:** Log in as `SUPER_ADMIN`
* **Step 1:** Look at the left-side navigation menu. Click on the link named **"Tenant Manager"**.
* **Step 2:** Type "QA Testing Corp" in the Company Name box. Type "qa-corp" in the Slug box. Click **Create Workspace**.
* **Expected Result:** A green success message (toast) should pop up on the screen.
* **Step 3:** Click the **Logout** button.
* **Expected Result:** You are kicked out of the app and sent back to the `/login` screen.

## 🔵 Phase 2: Company Management (Tenant Owner)

### Test 2.1: Tax Compliance (GSTIN Validation)

*Why we test this: Indian tax law requires exactly 15 characters. If we send bad data, billing fails.*

* **Role:** Log in as `TENANT_OWNER`
* **Step 1:** Click **"Settings"** in the left menu.
* **Step 2:** Find the GSTIN field. Type a bad ID: `123`. Click the **Save** button.
* **Expected Result:** The system should STOP you. It should highlight the box in red or show an error saying it must be 15 characters.
* **Step 3:** Now type exactly 15 characters: `12ABCDE3456F7Z8`. Select any State from the dropdown. Click **Save**.
* **Expected Result:** A success message appears. The data stays in the box even if you refresh the page.

### Test 2.2 & 2.3: Inviting a New Worker

*Why we test this: Bosses need to be able to safely bring their employees into the app.*

* **Role:** Still logged in as `TENANT_OWNER`
* **Step 1:** On the **Settings** page, scroll down to the "Team Invite" section.
* **Step 2:** Type a fake email (e.g., `qa-worker@test.com`) and click **Create Invite**.
* **Expected Result:** A long web link appears on the screen.
* **Step 3:** Click the **Copy** button next to that link.
* **Step 4:** Open a brand new **Incognito Window** (this acts like the worker opening their email). Paste the link into the address bar and press Enter.
* **Expected Result:** You are taken to a registration screen (`/invite/accept`).
* **Step 5:** Fill in a name and a password. Click **Save**.
* **Expected Result:** You are automatically redirected to the Login page. Log in with `qa-worker@test.com` and the password you just made. It should let you into the app!

### Test 2.4: The Billing Lockout

*Why we test this: If a company doesn't pay, we must freeze their ability to create new things.*

* **Role:** Log in as `TENANT_OWNER`
* **Pre-requisite:** *Ask a backend developer to set your test account's "AI Credits" to 0.*
* **Step 1:** Go to the CRM page. Click on any Deal card. Click the **"AI Score Deal"** button.
* **Expected Result:** The app must block you immediately. A big warning box should pop up saying your workspace is locked.
* **Step 2:** The app should automatically redirect your screen to the `/billing` page.
* **Step 3:** On the billing page, click **"Manage Billing (Portal)"**.
* **Expected Result:** You are taken out of the app and securely over to a Stripe payment page.

## 🟡 Phase 3: Worker Restrictions (Domain Member)

### Test 3.1: The URL Hacker Test (Route Guards)

*Why we test this: Normal workers shouldn't see company settings, even if they guess the web address.*

* **Role:** Log in as `DOMAIN_MEMBER` (Use the worker account you created in Test 2.3)
* **Step 1:** Look closely at the left menu.
* **Expected Result:** "Settings", "Billing", "LMS Author", and "Tenant Manager" MUST NOT be there.
* **Step 2 (The Hack):** Click on the top address bar. Delete whatever is there and type: `http://localhost:4200/settings` and hit Enter.
* **Expected Result:** The app catches you. It refuses to load the Settings page and kicks you back to the CRM or Login page.

### Test 3.2: Taking a Quiz (LMS Learner)

*Why we test this: Students must score 80% or higher to pass. (4 out of 5 correct).*

* **Role:** Log in as `DOMAIN_MEMBER`
* **Step 1:** Click **LMS Learner** in the menu. Enroll in a course.
* **Step 2:** Click **Take Quiz**. Answer only 2 questions correctly (less than 80%). Click Submit.
* **Expected Result:** The screen shows a red **FAIL**.
* **Step 3:** Take the quiz again. Answer 4 or 5 questions correctly (80%+). Click Submit.
* **Expected Result:** The screen shows a green **PASS**.

---

## 🟠 Phase 4: Content Creation (LMS Authoring)

### Test 4.1: Generating AI Quizzes

*Why we test this: Teachers should be able to press one button and let AI do the work.*

* **Role:** Log in as `TENANT_OWNER`
* **Step 1:** Click **LMS Author** in the menu.
* **Step 2:** Create a Course, then a Module, then a Lesson. Type some random text in the text editor box and save it.
* **Step 3:** Click the **"Generate AI Quiz"** button.
* **Expected Result:** The app doesn't freeze. You should see a progress bar showing that it's "thinking." After a few moments, 5 automatically generated quiz questions should appear on your screen.
